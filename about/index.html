ABOUT FLOCKWISE

Built to make poultry planning a little easier.

A straightforward app for the small calculations that make a difference in everyday farming decisions.

OUR STORY

It started with a practical question.

How can poultry keepers get useful planning figures without searching across spreadsheets, websites and complicated software?

Flockwise was created to answer that question. It brings common calculations for feed, water, housing, brooding and production costs into one accessible Android toolkit.

The goal is not to make farming decisions for you. It is to make the numbers behind those decisions easier to understand and use.

“Useful tools should be straightforward enough to use when there is real work to do.”

The idea behind Flockwise
